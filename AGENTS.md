# SaleRoar — Project Context for AI Agents

## What SaleRoar is
SaleRoar is a free weekly grocery deals newsletter and simple website for Oak Park, Illinois (zip codes 60301, 60302, 60304).
Every Wednesday it shows the best sale prices across five nearby stores, so shoppers know what to buy where without fighting store apps.

Stores covered (all within ~2 miles):
- Jewel-Osco (2 locations)
- Pete's Fresh Market (2 locations)
- Aldi (1 location)

## Who it's for
- Busy families who want to save on groceries without spending hours comparing.
- Seniors and anyone frustrated by the Jewel app and its digital "clip" coupons.

## Why now
- Shoppers report Jewel digital coupons are confusing and unreliable.
- A new Illinois law (HB 45, Public Act 104-0476) takes effect January 1. Stores with apps must give digital promotions to any eligible shopper through a practical method (automatic discount, barcode, QR code). This creates confusion and news coverage SaleRoar can help explain.

## Current stage and goal
- Stage: validation. Newsletter + one-page website. No mobile app yet.
- Goal: 100–200 local subscribers in the first 30 days, with good weekly open rates.
- Later ideas (only after validation): receipt checker for missed discounts, shopping basket, auto-clipping Jewel deals, nearby towns (River Forest, Forest Park, Berwyn, Elmwood Park).

## Weekly workflow
1. Tuesday night / Wednesday morning: collect the five stores' weekly ads.
2. Extract sale items into a table: category, item, size/unit, price, store, sale dates, digital coupon needed (yes/no).
3. Convert to comparable units (per lb, per dozen, per oz) and mark the cheapest store.
4. Pick the Top 5 deals for a typical family and a "smart trip" split (what to buy at which store).
5. Draft the issue: subject line, intro, Top 5, category table, tip of the week, footer.
6. Owner reviews and approves before anything is sent or posted.

## Tone and rules
- Friendly, neighborly, short. Playful energy is fine ("Yaaasss, it's on sale").
- Accuracy above everything. Never guess a price, size or date. If unsure, flag it for the owner.
- Neutral: no store is favored. Not affiliated with any store.
- Plain words. Many readers are not tech-savvy.

## Safety and permissions
- Always ask the owner before sending emails, posting publicly, publishing the website, or spending money.
- Never handle payment cards or buy anything. The owner does purchases.
- Never use the owner's personal store, bank or email logins. Use separate accounts only when the owner sets them up.
- Do not store or share shoppers' personal data beyond what the newsletter tool needs.
